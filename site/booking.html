CLOUD STUDIO XR / DAW ORIENTATION

Connect your DAW and join the right room

Reset Media Studios voice room is the permanent orientation link. Claim Your Stage is a separate voice room for performances, not paid engineering. An invitation is never proof of payment or permission to enter a private session.

  1. Connect headphones and a microphone/interface; open your DAW such as MPC or Reason. Use a 48 kHz audio session where supported.
  2. In Discord select your microphone or intentional Wave Link Stream Mix as input; use headphones as output.
  3. Share DAW audio through the intended input mix only. Keep incoming Discord audio out of the Discord-send bus (mix-minus) to prevent echo and feedback.
  4. Sound-check with the host: voice plus beat heard once, no doubled or delayed return.
  5. Record locally by default. Obtain explicit consent from everyone captured before recording, OBS capture or streaming. Deliver files privately with rights and sample clearance checked.

Service and payment terms: Self-led personal creation does not include a dedicated engineer, guaranteed room or equipment rental. Founder/engineer assistance, production and editing are scheduled and priced separately, subject to written scope, duration, deliverables, revisions, cancellation/refund terms and confirmed availability. Stripe Studio payments below are project deposits, not automatic bookings.
